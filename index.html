MARSA TERRAFORMING GAME

NEW COLONY
Goal: grow your outpost from a single pad to a thriving city.
• Build ☀️ power, 🌱 food and 🧊 water to keep colonists alive and happy.
• 🚀 Ships land on pads with new colonists — only if you have 🏠 housing.
• Every building costs $ credits + 🧱 materials. ⛏️ Mines dig ore, 🏗️ Processors turn it into materials; 🤖 Optimus robots carry ore from mines to processors and help build.
• Supply ships land on 🛬 pads every ~1.5 Sols with crew + materials. Order extra materials in the 🌍 Market.
• ⛽ Fuel plants turn water + power into fuel. Tap a landed Starship, refuel it, load surplus cargo and launch a return flight to sell on Earth.
• Paths are free and optional — buildings work anywhere; crew and rovers prefer walking on paths.
• Tap a building to rename / upgrade it, tap an astronaut to meet them.
• 🌷 Decorations near paths make colonists happier. Buildings light up at night.
• Bigger buildings (Waste, Medical, Entertainment, Security…) unlock as your population grows.
Controls: drag / WASD pan · wheel / pinch zoom · Q/E rotate 90° · pick a tool then tap/drag the map · two-finger drag pans on touch · press & hold a building to move it · right-click / Esc cancel.
