MARS OUTPOSTTYCOON

Goal: grow your outpost from a single pad to a thriving city.
• Build ☀️ power, 🌱 food and 🧊 water to keep colonists alive and happy.
• 🚀 Ships land on pads with new colonists — only if you have 🏠 housing.
• ⛽ Fuel plants make fuel; departing ships buy it for big credits.
• Bigger buildings unlock as your population grows.
Controls: drag / WASD pan · wheel / pinch zoom · Q/E or ⟲⟳ rotate 90° · tap a building in the bar then tap the map · press & hold a building to move it · right-click / Esc cancel.
